Pulsation puts a focused metronome on your Apple Watch, so your tempo is right there when you practice.

SET YOUR TEMPO

• Tap the tempo hint to estimate your pace.
• Follow the beat with a clear visual pulse.

PRACTICE WITH INTENTION
• Free keeps a steady beat at your chosen tempo.
• Ramp gradually raises the tempo as you practice.
• Silence adds quiet stretches to help you keep time internally.

CHOOSE YOUR CUES
• Turn the metronome click on or off.
• Turn wrist haptics on or off.
• Change sound and haptics independently in Settings.

Pulsation runs on Apple Watch and is made for quick, focused practice.

## App Review notes

```text
Pulsation is a watch-only metronome. Install and open it on Apple Watch. Use the Digital Crown or the on-screen plus and minus controls to change tempo. Tap the tempo hint to use tap tempo, then swipe vertically between Tempo, Practice, and Settings. Sound and haptics can be toggled independently. No sign-in is required.
```
